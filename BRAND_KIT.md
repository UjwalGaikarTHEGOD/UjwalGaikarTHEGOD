# ScreenStories Brand Kit (Yellow Identity)

## 1) Brand Foundation

ScreenStories uses **yellow-first branding** to signal confidence, energy, and optimism in a space that is usually dominated by dark palettes.

### Positioning statement
> "We’re bold, culturally aware, and clear-eyed about stories on screen—without hiding behind cinematic darkness."

## 2) Color System

### Primary brand yellow (recommended default)
- **Warm Film Yellow** — `#FFB703`
  - Chosen as the default because it balances retro cinema warmth and modern visual punch.

### Alternate yellow options (campaigns / A-B testing)
- **Mustard Yellow** — `#F4C430` (retro classic tone)
- **Golden Yellow** — `#FFC107` (brighter, energetic tone)

### Supporting colors
- **Charcoal Black** — `#1C1C1C` (text, navigation, grounding)
- **Cream White** — `#FFF8E1` (surfaces, soft page backgrounds)
- **Deep Brown** — `#3A2F1B` (secondary text, dividers, icon linework)
- **Alert Red** — `#B00020` (errors, destructive states only)

## 3) Practical Usage Ratios

To keep the brand premium (not noisy), use:
- **60% cream/white surfaces**
- **25% yellow background/accent planes**
- **10% charcoal text/structure**
- **5% brown + red utility accents**

## 4) UI Style Direction

### Visual language
- Rounded corners (12–20px radius on cards/containers)
- Bold typography with strong hierarchy
- Clean white cards over yellow background planes
- Subtle shadow depth (soft, not dramatic)
- Optional soft grain overlay for editorial/hero sections

### Spacing & density
- Generous spacing (8px grid)
- Keep sections breathable; avoid clutter
- Prefer fewer, larger elements over many small ones

## 5) Component Guidelines

### Cards
- Background: `#FFF8E1` or pure white
- Border: optional 1px `rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.08)`
- Radius: 16px
- Shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.08)`

### Buttons
- Primary: Yellow background + charcoal text
- Secondary: Cream background + charcoal border/text
- Danger: Red background + cream/white text

### Navigation
- Charcoal text/icons on yellow or cream surface
- Active state uses yellow underline or chip fill, not heavy effects

## 6) Brand Personality & Tone

### Personality
- Confident
- Energetic
- Optimistic
- Culturally relevant

### Voice rules
- Honest, never toxic
- Sharp, never arrogant
- Smart, never preachy

## 7) Logo Direction (Flat, high-contrast)

Preferred concepts to explore:
1. Solid yellow field + bold black wordmark “ScreenStories”
2. Film strip shape forming an “S”
3. Retro TV silhouette + modern typography
4. Minimal black circular badge + yellow typography

### Logo constraints
- Flat colors only (no gradients)
- Strong silhouette at small sizes
- 1-color variant must remain legible

## 8) Accessibility Baseline

- Minimum body text contrast: WCAG AA
- Do not place yellow text on cream backgrounds
- For small text, prefer charcoal on cream/white
- Reserve alert red for status and critical actions only
